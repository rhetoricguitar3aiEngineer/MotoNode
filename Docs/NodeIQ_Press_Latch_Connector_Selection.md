# NodeIQ field-wire connector review

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: stripped wire is inserted and released by hand at the assembled PCB; no field crimping or soldering is needed. The terminal pins are soldered to the PCB during board manufacture. Enclosure openings, strain relief and environmental sealing remain part of the mechanical design. This review is a selection input, not a claim that any unrouted board is ready for fabrication.

The inexpensive 5 mm Anytek QT terminal is rated 300 V and cannot be assigned to the nominal 350 V CDI output. A dielectric withstand test is not a continuous voltage rating. The manufacturer QT specification lists 600 V for its 10 mm-pitch family, while some distributor entries list particular 10 mm variants at 300 V; use the specific part's approval and drawing, not the family maximum, before substituting.

The bare-wire spring terminals do not make the overall motorcycle harness waterproof or release proof. The PCB enclosure, wire clamp access, strain relief, conductor gauge, fuse coordination and vibration/thermal qualification must be completed for a real installation. The user's no-crimp/no-solder requirement applies to field wiring, including the power board, hub, sensor boards and lighting boards.

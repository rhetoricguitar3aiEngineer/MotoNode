# MotoNodeIQ Launch Marketing Plan

> **Status: draft built on assumptions.** The repo contains no product information, so everything below assumes MotoNodeIQ is a **connected telemetry and analytics system for motorcycles**. That means a small bolt-on node plus an app for ride analytics, predictive maintenance, and theft/crash alerts.
> Anything in `[brackets]` is a placeholder. Replace it with real specs, pricing, and dates. If the product is something else, the structure still holds. The positioning and video content need a rewrite.

## 1. Goal and success metrics

| Horizon | Goal | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch (weeks -6 to 0) | Build a demand list | **[5,000] waitlist signups**, 35% from owned/earned channels |
| Launch (weeks 0 to 2) | Convert the list | **[600] first orders**, 12% waitlist-to-order conversion |

North-star metric: **cost per verified rider (waitlist signup) under [$6]**, and blended CAC under [$45].

## 2. Positioning

**One-liner:** *MotoNodeIQ turns every ride into intelligence: know your bike's health, your riding, and where it is, before something goes wrong.*

**Three pillars (each one anchors a video):**
1. **Know your bike.** Predictive maintenance: chain, tires, battery, and service intervals based on real usage.
2. **Ride smarter.** Lean angle, braking, and corner analytics, with track-day and commuter modes.
3. **Never lose it.** Theft movement alerts, live GPS, and crash detection with an emergency contact.

**Proof points to collect before launch** (credibility is the main risk in the hardware category): [N]-rider beta, [X,000] logged miles, one real "caught it early" story, install time of under [15 min], and [battery life] claim backed by a test video.

**Tone:** Rider-to-rider, technical but not nerdy, with no fear-mongering.

## 3. Audience segments

| Segment | Hook | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekend sport/track riders** | Lap and corner analytics, tire and brake data | r/motorcycles, track-day orgs, YouTube moto creators |
| **Adventure/touring riders** | Maintenance prediction, remote-area crash alert | ADVrider, Instagram, long-distance YouTubers |
| **Commuters/urban riders** | Theft alerts, service reminders | TikTok, local clubs, insurance comparison sites |

Lead with the first two. They are the loudest advocates and generate the content the rest of the funnel needs.

## 5. Channel plan

**Owned**
- **Landing page and waitlist:** embed the flagship video above the fold. Track video-watch to signup.
- **Email (the main conversion channel):** 8-email sequence: welcome, origin story, pillar x3, proof, early-bird, last call.
- **YouTube channel:** home of all 10-minute videos. Chapters, strong thumbnails, and searchable titles ("How to predict motorcycle maintenance with data").

**Earned**
- **Creator seeding (10 to 15):** mid-size moto YouTubers (20k to 300k). Free unit, no scripted talking points, embargo date set. Offer an affiliate code.
- **Press:** moto and tech press (RideApart, Cycle World, Motorcyclist, Gear Patrol, Hackaday if the hardware story is strong). Pitch an angle, not a product: *"What 1,000 hours of ride data says about when bikes fail."*
- **Communities:** Reddit, ADVrider, Discord. Participate first and promote second. Founder-led and transparent.

**Paid** (budget in section 7)
- YouTube in-stream and in-feed using video cutdowns, targeted by moto-interest and competitor channels.
- Meta and TikTok retargeting for site and video viewers.
- Google Search on high-intent terms ("motorcycle GPS tracker", "motorcycle maintenance tracker").

**Short-form engine:** every 10-minute video yields **8 to 10 cutdowns** (15 to 60 s) for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok, with 3 to 4 posts per week during weeks L-5 to L+4.

## 6. The 10-minute video series

Six videos, each **~10:00** (target 9:30 to 10:30), each built to stand alone and to feed the next. Formats are rider-first, with real footage over studio shots wherever possible.

| # | Title | Publish | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Why Motorcycles Break (and Nobody Warns You)"** | L-5 | Awareness: problem and origin story |
| 2 | **"Inside MotoNodeIQ: How It Actually Works"** | L-4 | Education: tech walkthrough |
| 3 | **"We Tried to Break It: 1,000 Miles of Torture Testing"** | L-3 | Credibility: proof |
| 4 | **"Riders Tell the Truth: Beta Stories"** | L-2 | Social proof |
| 5 | **"MotoNodeIQ Launch: The Full Reveal"** | L (launch day) | Conversion: the flagship |
| 6 | **"Install and Setup in 15 Minutes (Step by Step)"** | L+3 | Retention and support, evergreen SEO |

### Production standards (apply to all)
- **Format:** 4K, 24/30 fps, 16:9 master, with a 9:16 cutdown pass for short-form. Burned-in captions plus SRT upload.
- **Audio:** lav plus on-bike mic for ride footage, with clean room tone. Royalty-free licensed music only.
- **Structure rule:** hook inside 0:15, a payoff tease at 0:45, a retention spike (new scene, reveal, or stat) every 90 to 120 seconds, and a soft CTA at 8:30 (never before 3:00, aside from a pinned comment).
- **Thumbnail/title:** test 3 variants with YouTube's Test and Compare.
- **Runtime math:** a spoken script runs at about 140 words per minute, so 10 minutes is about 1,400 words of narration, assuming roughly 30% of runtime is b-roll with no voiceover.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hardware delay or supply shortage | Don't announce a ship date until a production unit is in hand. Use "ships [month]" wording with buffer. |
| Claims aren't backed (battery, accuracy, theft detection) | Every claim in a video must have its test footage or data on file. Legal reviews all spec claims. |
| Safety and liability language (crash detection) | State clearly that it is an aid, not a guarantee. Legal review before launch. |
| Data privacy concerns (location, riding data) | Privacy page live before Video 2. Plain-English policy and data export/delete. |
| Creator reviews turn negative | Seed honestly, fix issues found in beta first, and respond constructively. |
| Launch-day support overload | Help center and Video 6 prepared in advance. Staff a live chat. FAQ pinned. |
